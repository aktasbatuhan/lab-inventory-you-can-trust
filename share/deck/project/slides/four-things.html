Four things kept apart

Material

What something is

EGFR extracellular domain

Lot

One batch with a history: one delivery, or one making

delivery DN-9001 of supplier lot L4471

Container

A physical thing. A barcode is a label it wears for a while.

tube TGT-0001, well B7, box 7, freezer F1

Contents

How much of which lot is where. Never stored, always added up.

24 µL of lot 12 in ALQ-007

Keeping contents separate is what lets a well hold two lots, and an expression plate keep its barcode while its DNA turns into 96 new protein lots.

Most of the failures in the brief come from blurring these four. A barcode is not the container: labels fall off, get reprinted, get reused after disposal. A lot is not the supplier's lot number: suppliers reuse numbers, and the same lot can arrive twice, so each delivery is its own batch and waits for its own QC. Material types are rows with rules, not tables, so adding small molecules needs no schema change. One of the tests does exactly that.
