The mistakes in the scenarios use published error rates (a wrong tube scanned about once in 2,000 scans, 3.7% of typed values wrong). The lab they happen in is modelled on Adaptyv’s own docs, papers and job posts.

I wrote the scenarios before the schema, so the schema had something to be tested against. Agents expanded each area of lab life, a research agent found real error rates, and Codex played the adversary. Where agents disagreed, that was a design decision to make on purpose, and those decisions are written up in the repo. The 25 shortlisted scenarios became the tests.
