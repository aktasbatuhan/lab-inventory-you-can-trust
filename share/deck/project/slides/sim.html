What stayed wrong

sim/ and the Simulations view of the review console, where every run and every mistake can be browsed

The point isn't a single number. It's that the design was tested against the kind of mess a real lab makes, at the scale Adaptyv runs. The simulation also tested the schema's speed: at four weeks of data, the "what's usable" question took 77 seconds. Profiling found two lookups that ran once per row, and the fix made it 1.5 seconds with identical answers. What stays silently wrong is honest: if nobody scans a bin, no system can know. The answer there is to make the common case automatic, as we did for single-use plates, and to surface things nobody has seen in a while.
