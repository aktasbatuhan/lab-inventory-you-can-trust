Two times on every fact

When it happened at the bench, and when it was written down. Facts are never edited or deleted: a mistake is fixed by a correction that points at it, and the wrong entry stays, marked as corrected.

Every answer in the system takes these two times. The highlighted row is the brief’s “what did the record say before the change?”

Every calculated function takes two dates. The first is the moment in the lab we're asking about. The second is how much of the record to believe, by when it was written down. That one idea answers what is true now, what the system said at the time, and whether something will still be good on the day of a planned re-run. It also makes late scans and back-dated entries safe: they land in the right place in history without rewriting what people saw at the time.
