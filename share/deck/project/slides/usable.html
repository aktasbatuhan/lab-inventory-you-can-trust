This is the brief's first question and the procurement person's question: before reordering an expensive target, how much is really left? The honest answer is two numbers and the reasons for the gap. In the demo lab the gap is mostly dead volume across many small aliquots, which is itself a finding: the aliquot size wastes target. A warning never blocks; a reason does.
