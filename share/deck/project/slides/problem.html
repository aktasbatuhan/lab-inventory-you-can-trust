The inventory is what everything else trusts

“Recording the facts correctly matters more than any single feature.”

From the brief. I read the case as a data-integrity problem first and an inventory problem second.

Owned material gets reordered

because the records say it’s used up

Plates in the bin look usable

because nobody recorded throwing them away

A bad batch can’t be traced

to the experiments it touched

These three costs are straight from the brief. Each one is a case of the records drifting away from what really happened, and nobody being able to tell. So the question I designed around was: how do you keep the records honest when many hands, robots and scanners write to them, and people make mistakes?
